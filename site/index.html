Detection-as-code · Sigma · ATT&CK

30 rules. 47 attack scenarios caught. 3 false positives in 1.19M clean events.

Every push lints the rules, converts them to Splunk, Elastic and Microsoft KQL with pySigma, then replays public attack captures and clean Windows baselines through them. A rule that misses its attack or goes over its noise budget fails the build.
